Paint Your Own Vinyl

Hands-on workshop

Presented by Foster Art

Let's Felt! Ornaments

Hands-on workshop
Thursday, December 3 · 6:00pm-8:00pm

Presented by Foster Art
1770 Timberwood Blvd, Ste 106, Charlottesville, VA, 22911

Every year, we look for new ways to connect, and make something special for the holidays. We book the glass-blowing classes, or scour the internet for the perfect project that offers something a little different to make with our own hands. This year, why not try felting? In this Let's Felt! workshop, you'll create your own felted ornaments by adding colorful wool designs of your choice to 3D felt spheres. Whether you go traditional, or whimsical, you'll leave with a fun new ornament, ready to become a new part of your holiday traditions. They make beautiful decorations, as well as thoughtful handmade gifts. No previous felting experience is needed. Come enjoy some hot cocoa, try something new, and Let's Felt!
